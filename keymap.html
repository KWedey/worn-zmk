How the layers open

Lower+Raise →Number pad

Hold the left inner thumb for Lower, the right inner thumb for Raise. Hold both at once and ZMK swaps you to the number pad — that is the tri_layer block in the keymap.

Reading a key

  • Solid — this layer sets the key
  • Dashed — falls through; the faint legend is what you actually get
  • Copper — layer key, held not tapped
  • Blue — arrows
  • Violet — Bluetooth profile
